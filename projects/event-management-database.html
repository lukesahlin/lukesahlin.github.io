Event Management System Database

A normalized PostgreSQL design for organizations that run recurring events: registrations, venue capacity, vendor relationships, and staff responsibilities—modeled with integrity constraints and role-aware access patterns.

Back to portfolio

Requirements

Event operations generate competing priorities: marketing wants flexibility, finance wants auditability, and on-site teams need truth about headcount and assignments. The schema reflects those tensions explicitly.

Design choices

  • Third-normal-form core tables for events, sessions, attendees, vendors, and staff.
  • Referential integrity and check constraints to prevent orphan registrations or double-booked resources.
  • Role-based access patterns aligned with planners, finance readers, and vendors with scoped visibility.
  • Indexing strategy notes for high-read paths (sign-in lookups, schedule boards).

Course context

Developed as part of database coursework: ER modeling through migrations, with emphasis on documenting assumptions and tradeoffs for future engineers—not just tables on a diagram.
