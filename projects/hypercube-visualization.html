Hypercube Visualization

A browser-based exploration of a four-dimensional hypercube (tesseract): vertices, edges, and faces projected into 2D so you can build intuition for higher-dimensional geometry.

GitHub Back to portfolio

Concept

Humans perceive three dimensions directly; everything beyond is convention and math. Projective transforms make four-dimensional objects understandable by animating rotations and projections in real time in the canvas or DOM.

Implementation

  • Pure HTML/JavaScript—no heavyweight game engine—so the logic stays visible.
  • Linear algebra routines for rotation matrices and perspective projection steps.
  • Interactive controls to orbit the shape and compare projections.

Takeaway

Small graphics projects are excellent practice for matrix fluency, numerical stability instincts, and explaining technical ideas to non-experts through motion instead of equations alone.
