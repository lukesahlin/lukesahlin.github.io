Three Classical Ciphers

Readable Python implementations of Caesar, Vigenère, and one-time pad encryption—aimed at understanding classical constructions before modern primitives and protocols.

GitHub Back to portfolio

Scope

Each cipher is implemented as its own small module with clear boundaries: key handling, encryption, decryption, and edge cases like non-alphabetic characters or key reuse (where pedagogically relevant).

Quality bar

  • Unit tests covering round-trips, rotated alphabets, and failure modes.
  • Design notes on why these schemes are obsolete for real secrecy—and what attacks target them.
  • Demonstrations suitable for walkthroughs or coursework presentation.

Why classical crypto still matters

Modern cryptography builds on formal definitions and proofs; classical ciphers are where many people first meet keys, entropy, and the difference between obscurity and security. This repo is my structured tour through that foundation.
